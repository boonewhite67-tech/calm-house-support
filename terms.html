The service

Calm House is a calm, ADHD-friendly family cleaning schedule for parents with kids. It covers daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual cleaning without a piled-up list. A parent creates an account with email and password, or signs in with the same account as House Points. One parent account belongs to one household. The whole household shares that one home.

A parent sets up the home once. Today shows a short list. Tasks can use a timer. Good Enough is a low-energy mode that keeps the essentials. Catch-up offers a short list instead of a backlog. Pause can hold the house for a few days. Child phones join with an eight-character family code. That code adds a child device. It does not create another parent account.

Parents can link Calm House to House Points so a finished chore assigned to a child can award points. A parent check-off adds the points. A child check-off asks a parent to approve them in House Points. Points are a family score in House Points. They have no cash value, are not money, and cannot be redeemed with Boone White or Apple.

Household data is stored in Supabase. The parent is responsible for the home details, tasks, and child profiles they enter, and for who is given the family code.

Accounts and deletion

You need a parent account to create a household. Keep the password private. You can reset the password from the sign-in screen.

A parent can permanently delete the account in Settings → Delete account. That deletes the family that parent created. Because the family is shared with House Points, this removes that family’s House Points data, including child profiles, points, rewards, and requests, and the Calm House home, tasks, and completions, and then deletes the parent login. A child phone can remove its own access with Remove this phone. That removal leaves the household schedule and points in place.
