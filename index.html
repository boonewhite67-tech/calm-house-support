Calm House

A calm, ADHD-friendly family cleaning schedule for parents with kids. The bundle id is com.boonewhite.calmhouse.

What the app is

Calm House keeps a household cleaning plan that covers daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual work without turning it into a pile. The whole household shares one home. Parents set it up once. Today stays short.

Today

Home setup asks whether the place is an apartment or a house, how many bedrooms and bathrooms it has, whether there are pets or kids, and the house timezone. That fills a starter list. Today shows a short list for that timezone. Check one off, or start a task timer. The timer keeps its start time, so the countdown still finishes if the app is in the background.

A lighter day

Good Enough is a low-energy mode: only the essentials, and the rest can wait. Missed days do not pile up. Catch-up offers a short list, at most three jobs from earlier this week, and a parent can let the rest start fresh on their next day. Pause holds the house for a few days.

One household

Parents sign in with email and password. The same account and the same family are shared with House Points. A child phone joins with the eight-character family code. Parents can link Calm House to House Points so a finished chore can award a child points.

How a household starts

  1. On the parent phone, sign in, or choose Create a family, make a parent account, and name the family. Confirm the email if the app asks you to. An account belongs to one family.
  2. Set up the home once. Calm House fills a starter list for daily, weekly, and longer cleaning.
  3. Use Today for the short list. Open the schedule to see daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual tasks. Parents can edit the list. Children can look, and can check off jobs that are open to them.
  4. On a child’s phone, choose Join with a family code and enter the eight-character code from House Points. That phone joins as a child. It does not get a parent account.
  5. Optional: in Settings, turn on Award House Points. A parent check-off of an assigned chore adds the points right away. A child check-off asks a parent to approve them in House Points.

Free and Premium

Free

Daily, weekly, and monthly jobs, task timers, Good Enough, catch-up, and pause. The Today list stays available without a subscription.

Calm House Premium

$2.99 per month or $19.99 per year in the United States. The yearly plan has a 2-week free trial. One parent subscription covers the household: quarterly, semi-annual, and annual deep cleans, the declutter list, and reminder notifications at chosen times.

Apple charges the parent’s Apple ID. The subscription renews until it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Restore a purchase from the Premium screen in the app. Reminder notifications are local notifications scheduled on the device. Full terms are on the Terms of Use page.

FAQ

What does Premium add?

Calm House Premium unlocks quarterly, semi-annual, and annual deep cleans, the declutter list, and reminder notifications at a time the parent chooses. Daily, weekly, and monthly jobs, timers, Good Enough, catch-up, and pause stay on the free plan. One subscription covers every parent and child phone in that household.

What is Good Enough?

Good Enough is the low-energy mode. Today keeps the essential jobs and lets the rest wait. It is there for days when the full list would be too much.

What happens if we miss a day?

Missed zone days do not stack into a long backlog. Catch-up shows a short list, at most three jobs from earlier in the week. A parent can let the others start fresh on their next day. Pause can hold the house for a few days.

How do finished chores award House Points?

The household is the same family as House Points. A parent can turn on Award House Points and assign a chore to a child. When a parent checks that chore off, the points are added. When a child checks it off, Calm House asks a parent to approve the points in House Points. The points are a family score in House Points. They are not money.

What can a child phone do?

It joins with the eight-character family code and can see the household list and check off jobs that are open to that phone. It cannot buy Calm House Premium. A parent subscribes, and the child phone is included. Remove this phone in Settings signs that phone out and removes its access. The family’s schedule and points stay.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot your password? The app emails a reset link to the parent account.

How do I delete my account and data?

On the parent phone, open Settings and choose Delete account. That permanently deletes the family that parent created. The family is shared with House Points, so this also deletes that family’s House Points data, including child profiles, points, and rewards, and the Calm House home, tasks, and completions. It then deletes the parent account. You can also email boone-white67-jj3gjy@mail.grokbot.com and ask for the account and its data to be deleted. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel that in iPhone Settings.

Does the app show ads or sell our information?

No. Calm House has no ads, does not sell personal information, and does not use third-party tracking. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
