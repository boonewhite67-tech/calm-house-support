Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026. This policy describes the iOS app Calm House (bundle id com.boonewhite.calmhouse), operated by Boone White.

Calm House does not show ads, does not sell personal information, and does not use third-party tracking.

Who this covers

A parent creates the account and the child profiles. Calm House uses the same parent account and the same household as House Points. A child phone joins with a family code and uses an anonymous sign-in. The parent decides what is written about a child. The app does not ask children for an email address.

Information the app stores

Account and household data is stored in Supabase so the family can sign in and share one home.

Parent account Email address, password (handled by the authentication service), display name, and a user id. Used to sign in, recover a password, and keep the household together.
Household The shared family name and eight-character invite code, plus the Calm House home: apartment or house, bedroom and bathroom counts, whether there are pets or kids, timezone, Good Enough or regular energy mode, pause, whether finished chores award House Points, the default points, and a reminder time if the parent set one.
Children Profiles the parent creates and manages: name or nickname, point balance, and avatar emoji. Children’s data is not a separate child email account.
Schedule Rooms and zones, cleaning tasks, and completions, including the day, the outcome, and who checked the task off. If House Points awards are on, a completion can record the points given or the approval request sent to House Points.
Child device An anonymous user id for that phone, linked to the household after the code is entered. It is used so this phone can see the shared home. It is not the child’s name or email.
Subscription Whether the household has Calm House Premium, the product (monthly or yearly), renewal information, and the family id used to unlock every phone in that household. Apple charges the Apple ID. The app does not receive the card number.

On the device

The signed-in session is kept in encrypted storage on the phone. A task timer stores its start time on the device so the countdown can finish if the app is in the background.

Reminder notifications are local notifications scheduled on the device for a time the parent chooses. The chosen time is saved with the household. Calm House does not send those reminders as remote push notifications, and it does not use them for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts authentication and the household database, including the shared House Points family.
  • Apple processes the in-app purchase and provides the App Store subscription.
  • RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and the family id so Calm House Premium can be confirmed for every phone in the household. RevenueCat is used for subscriptions, not for advertising.

These providers process data to run the app. Household information is not sold, and it is not used for third-party tracking or advertising.

How long it is kept

Household data is kept while the parent account and family exist.

When a parent deletes the account in the app, the family that parent created is deleted. That removes the shared House Points family data, including child profiles, points, rewards, and requests, and the Calm House home, tasks, and completions, and then deletes the parent login. A record of a subscription event may remain without the family id so a purchase can still be reconciled. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their policies.

Removing a child phone deletes that phone’s anonymous access. It does not delete the household schedule, the family’s points, or the parent account.

Account and data deletion

Parents can delete the account in the app: Settings → Delete account. That deletion covers the shared family used by Calm House and House Points. Anyone can also email boone-white67-jj3gjy@mail.grokbot.com and ask Boone White to delete the account and its data. Include the parent email on the account.

Deleting the Calm House account does not cancel Calm House Premium. Cancel the subscription in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Children’s data

Child profiles are created and managed by the parent. A child phone does not have its own email account inside the app. The parent can delete the whole family as described above.
